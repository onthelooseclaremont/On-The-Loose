Hiking / Backpacking — San Gabriel Front Range

Mt. Lowe Loop

A history-rich loop through the ruins of the old Mt. Lowe Railway, one of the most unusual hikes in the front range.

This loop climbs from Echo Mountain — the ruined site of a turn-of-the-century mountain resort served by an incline railway — up through the site of the old Alpine Tavern to Mt. Lowe's summit, then loops back down through Millard Canyon. The ruins along the way (rail beds, foundations, an old dance hall pad) make it one of the more historically interesting front-range hikes.

It's a solid full-day loop and can also be split into an easier out-and-back to Echo Mountain alone for a shorter trip.

Trailhead

Sam Merrill Trailhead, Lake Avenue, Altadena, CA — about 45 minutes from Claremont.
