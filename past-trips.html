Past trips

recent reports

Channel Islands x FLI Scholars

DEST — Prisoners Harbor, Santa Cruz Island LEADER — OTL x FLI Scholars DATE — Apr 18–19

A co-hosted weekend with FLI Scholars — hiking and hot springs in the Los Padres, a night camped at Emma Wood State Beach, then a ferry out to a quiet cove on Santa Cruz Island to hike, swim, and snorkel.

Conserving Celestial Catalina

DEST — Catalina Island Conservancy LEADER — Alida & Anna DATE — Apr 2–5

A conservation and service trip run with the Catalina Island Conservancy — trail work, invasive plant removal, and time in the native plant nursery, with hiking and beach time worked in around it.

Spring Break in Big Sur

DEST — Big Sur, Plaskett Creek Campground LEADER — Casey & Charlotte DATE — Mar 15–19

Five days of backpacking the Big Sur coast — camped at Plaskett Creek, a soak at Sykes Hot Springs, sunset at Sand Dollar Beach, and a final day exploring nearby waterfalls before the drive home.

The Sky's More Blue... In Malibu

DEST — Malibu Creek State Park & Point Dume LEADER — Grace & Izzy DATE — Mar 7

A 6.4-mile moderate hike through Malibu Creek State Park, followed by an afternoon at Point Dume — boogie boards, sunscreen, and a beach to end the day on.

Backpacking Mt. Wilson and Beyond

DEST — Chantry Flats — Mt. Wilson & Mt. Zion LEADER — Maya & Chloe DATE — Feb 20–22

Eighteen tough miles over three days out of Chantry Flats, summiting both Mt. Wilson and Mt. Zion with serious elevation gain along the way — built for backpackers ready for a real challenge.

Catalina Lobster Fishing Extravaganza

DEST — Catalina Island, via San Pedro LEADER — OTL x POCO x PEC DATE — Feb 20–21

A first-of-its-kind chartered trip — a full day on the water fishing and setting lobster pots off Catalina, with the catch prepared by Pomona Dining for the whole club to share afterward.
