Big Bear Art & Wander Day

DEST — Big Bear LEADER — Frank L. DATE — Feb

Drove up, found comfy rocks, spent the day sketching and painting the lake. Low-key, high scenery.

Mammoth Powder Weekend

DEST — Mammoth Lakes LEADER — Andrew Y. DATE — Feb

A foot of fresh snow, a full van, and a very tired drive home Sunday night. Worth every mile.

Snow Summit Snowboarding

DEST — Big Bear, Snow Summit LEADER — Naomi F. DATE — Feb

Early start, good conditions, back for dinner. A few first-timers left standing up more than they fell.

Sunset Ridge Trail Run

DEST — San Gabriel foothills LEADER — Caitlin G. DATE — Jan

A short, sweaty loop timed to catch the sunset from the ridge. Coffee after at the Motley.

Tahquitz Rock Climbing

DEST — Idyllwild LEADER — Anna K. DATE — Nov

Multi-pitch practice for the more experienced crew, top-roping for everyone else. Camped at the base overnight.

Bolsa Chica Coastal Walk

DEST — Huntington Beach wetlands LEADER — Billy M. DATE — Oct

A flat, easy afternoon out — birdwatching, tide pools, and a very good burrito stop on the way back.
