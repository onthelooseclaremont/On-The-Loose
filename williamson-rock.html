Williamson Rock

A well-known granite sport and trad crag near Mt. Williamson — historically one of the best in the San Gabriels, subject to periodic seasonal closures.

Williamson Rock, near Mt. Williamson on the Angeles Crest Highway, has long been regarded as one of the best granite crags in the San Gabriels — high-quality rock and a good range of grades. Access has been intermittently restricted over the years to protect mountain yellow-legged frog habitat in the area.

Before planning a trip here, check current Angeles National Forest access status — closures have come and gone over the years and current conditions should be confirmed rather than assumed.

Check access first: this area has a history of seasonal or extended closures for habitat protection. Confirm current status before a trip.

Trailhead

Angeles Crest Highway near Islip Saddle — about 1.5 hours from Claremont.
