Joshua Tree

OTL's most-visited destination — camping, hiking, and some of the best beginner-to-advanced climbing in the Southwest, all two hours from Claremont.

Joshua Tree is the closest thing OTL has to a home base outside Claremont — close enough for a weekend trip, big enough that regulars are still finding new areas. The park splits roughly into hiking terrain (Lost Horse Valley, the Wonderland of Rocks) and climbing areas (Hidden Valley, Ryan Campground, Echo Rock, and dozens more), with thousands of routes across every grade.

It's also just a great place to camp — clear skies, mild winters, and short approaches mean you can fit a lot into a weekend without long drives between activities.

Camping: several campgrounds are reservation-only and fill up months out for busy weekends. Book early or plan on a first-come, first-served site and a backup plan.

Trailhead

West or South Entrance off Highway 62 — about 2 hours from Claremont via the 10.
