Sequoia & Kings Canyon

Giant sequoia groves, deep granite canyons, and some of the best backpacking access in the southern Sierra.

Sequoia and Kings Canyon are managed together and cover an enormous range of terrain, from the General Sherman Tree (the largest tree on Earth by volume) to Kings Canyon, one of the deepest canyons in the country, to high alpine backcountry along the Sierra crest.

Day-trip groups usually stick to the Giant Forest and Grant Grove areas; backpacking groups head into the high country from trailheads like Mineral King or Road's End, both of which require wilderness permits.

Trailhead

Giant Forest Museum (Sequoia) or Road's End (Kings Canyon) — about 4.5 hours from Claremont via the 99 and 198.
