Upcoming Trips

Difficulty is marked low to high with trail-blaze dots. Show up, or email the leader if you have questions.

Icehouse Canyon Day Hike

DEST — Icehouse Canyon to the Saddle LEADER — Caitlin G. DATE — Sat, 8:00 AM

An 8-mile round trip up one of the prettiest canyons near campus. Steady climb, big payoff at the top. Snacks and layers recommended — it's cold at the saddle.

Baldy Snow Scramble

DEST — Mt. Baldy LEADER — Frank L. DATE — Sat–Sun

Fresh snow up top after last week's storms. Microspikes provided from the gear room, but bring your own warm layers. Turnaround time is non-negotiable.

Joshua Tree Anchor Clinic

DEST — Joshua Tree National Park LEADER — Anna K. DATE — Fri PM – Sat

Leave Friday evening, camp under a very good sky, spend Saturday building and testing anchors with the climbing staff. Open to all experience levels.

Marshall Canyon Bike Loop

DEST — Marshall Canyon Regional Park LEADER — Naomi F. DATE — Sun, 9:00 AM

Rolling singletrack about 30 minutes from campus. We've got a few loaner bikes — first come, first served. Back in time for dinner.

San Jacinto Overnight

DEST — San Jacinto Peak LEADER — Andrew Y. DATE — Next weekend

Tram up, hike in, camp near the peak, watch the sunset drop over the desert floor. Limited to eight people — sign up early.

Full Moon Desert Walk

DEST — Mojave Preserve LEADER — Billy M. DATE — TBD, next full moon

No headlamps needed — that's the whole point. A slow evening walk under a very bright moon, followed by hot chocolate at the cars.
