Climbing — San Gabriel Canyon

Big Rock

A roadside crag along Highway 39, good for an easy after-class or half-day climbing trip close to campus.

Big Rock is a convenient, low-commitment crag along San Gabriel Canyon Road (Highway 39) — short approach, a range of grades, and close enough to Claremont for an after-class trip rather than a full day out.

It's a good spot for OTL to run beginner top-roping sessions, since the approach is minimal and there's enough terrain to set up multiple stations.

Trailhead

San Gabriel Canyon Road (Hwy 39), north of Azusa — about 40 minutes from Claremont.
