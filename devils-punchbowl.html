Devil's Punchbowl

A dramatic tilted-rock formation on the desert edge of the San Gabriels, with hiking loops and some scrambling on the sandstone.

Devil's Punchbowl is a striking formation of tilted, uplifted sandstone at the desert edge of the San Gabriels, formed along the San Andreas Fault. The county natural area has short interpretive loop trails good for a half-day trip, while a longer backpacking loop connects the Punchbowl to the nearby South Fork of Little Rock Creek for a fuller overnight.

The rock formations invite some light scrambling, but this isn't a technical climbing destination — bring good shoes and expect loose, sandy terrain.

Trailhead

Devil's Punchbowl Natural Area, off Fort Tejon Road, Pearblossom, CA — about 1.5 hours from Claremont.
