San Gabriel Foothills Trails

A network of foothill singletrack and fire roads stretching along the base of the San Gabriels — good for a shorter ride close to campus.

The foothill trail network running along the base of the San Gabriels — through areas like San Dimas Canyon, Marshall Canyon, and the Glendora/Duarte foothills — offers a range of terrain from mellow fire roads to rockier singletrack, all within a short drive of campus.

This is OTL's go-to for a lower-commitment ride, good for beginners and experienced riders looking for something quick after class.

Trailhead

Trailheads vary — Marshall Canyon Regional Park and San Dimas Canyon are common starting points, both about 30–45 minutes from Claremont.
