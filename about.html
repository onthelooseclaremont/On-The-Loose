Who we are

About OTL

On The Loose is the student-run outdoors club of the Claremont Colleges — a volunteer crew of trip leaders keeping the 5Cs outside.

The short version

OTL exists because Southern California's mountains, deserts, and coastline are right outside the classroom door, and getting out to them shouldn't require years of experience. We're entirely student-run: student trip leaders and a student-written guide to where to go.

Every trip is planned and led by a fellow 5C student who's been through our leader training. That keeps things informal, keeps trips free or close to it, and means the person leading your hike is someone you'll see in the dining hall afterward.

How it runs

Anyone can propose a trip. Leaders post a destination, a date, and a headcount; interested folks sign up. Leaders swap trip reports and train new leaders along the way.

You don't need to be an experienced hiker, climber, or camper to come along — most trips are built for a mix of first-timers and regulars, and leaders adjust pace and difficulty to the group.

Getting involved

Come to a Fireside Chat, stop by our table at the club fair each October, or just join a trip with no strings attached. If you want to lead trips yourself, ask about the next leader training — we run wilderness first aid and navigation sessions most semesters.
