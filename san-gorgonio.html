Hiking / Backpacking — San Bernardino Mountains

San Gorgonio

The highest peak in Southern California, and one of the biggest single-day elevation gains you'll find in the region.

"Old Grayback" is the tallest mountain in Southern California, and the Vivian Creek Trail is the shortest, steepest, and most popular way up — a serious full-day effort that regularly serves as training for Mt. Whitney. Expect thinning air and exposed, sun-baked switchbacks in the final miles.

For a backpacking version, the South Fork Trail via Dry Lake and Dollar Lake is a longer but gentler approach, with established wilderness campsites that make a two-day version of the summit much more manageable.

Permit: a wilderness permit is required for San Gorgonio Wilderness (South Fork, Vivian Creek, and Momyer trailheads), available through the San Gorgonio Wilderness Association.

Trailhead

Vivian Creek Trailhead, Forest Falls, CA — about 1.5 hours from Claremont via the 10 and 38.
