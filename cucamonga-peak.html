Hiking — San Gabriel Mountains / Cucamonga Wilderness

Cucamonga Peak

One of the best-view summits in the San Gabriels, reached via the same trailhead as Icehouse Canyon.

Cucamonga Peak shares its trailhead with Icehouse Canyon but keeps climbing past Icehouse Saddle to a summit widely considered to have the best views of any peak in the San Gabriels — the Inland Empire on one side, the high country on the other. It's part of the local "Six-Pack of Peaks" challenge circuit.

The north-facing final stretch holds snow well into late spring even when the rest of the range is clear — check conditions and bring microspikes if there's been recent snow.

Permit: a free, self-serve wilderness permit is required for the Cucamonga Wilderness, available at the trailhead.

Trailhead

Icehouse Canyon trailhead, Mt. Baldy Road — parking fills by 6–7am on weekends.
