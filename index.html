What we run

free & student-run
→ Trips

Weekend & day trips

Student-led hikes, climbs, rides, and overnights across the San Gabriels, the desert, and beyond — most weekends, all skill levels.

→ Gear room

Equipment loans

Tents, packs, stoves, and cold-weather gear, free to borrow for any registered 5C student — trip or no trip.

→ Guide

The OTL trip guide

A student-written, student-updated guide to the best routes within a few hours of Claremont — where to go and what to expect.

→ Training

Leader certification

Want to lead your own trip? We run wilderness first aid, navigation, and leadership training every semester.
