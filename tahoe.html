Tahoe

The big trip — a full ski week's worth of terrain across a dozen-plus resorts, and a genuine road trip from Claremont.

Tahoe is on a different scale than anything else in this guide — a dozen-plus resorts ringing the lake, from Squaw Valley/Alpine Meadows and Northstar on the north shore to Heavenly on the south, with enough terrain to fill a full week. This is exclusively a multi-day OTL trip given the drive.

Because it's such a large undertaking, Tahoe trips are usually planned well in advance, with lodging booked early for winter break or a long weekend.

Trailhead

Varies by resort — the drive from Claremont is roughly 8 hours via the 5 and 80.
