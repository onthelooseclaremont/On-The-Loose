Hiking — San Bernardino National Forest

Deep Creek Hot Springs

A steep drop into a canyon to a set of natural mineral pools along Deep Creek — the climb back out is the real workout.

Deep Creek Hot Springs sits along a scenic stretch of the Pacific Crest Trail in the high desert above Hesperia — three natural pools of varying temperature right along the creek. Several approach trails exist (Bowen Ranch, Bradford Ridge, and the PCT itself); the Bowen Ranch route is the shortest.

Whichever way you go down is the way you climb back up — the descent is steep and loose, and the return climb in the heat of the day is the hardest part of the trip.

Water quality: the creek has tested with elevated bacteria counts at times — treat any water you filter from it, and don't count on the springs themselves being pristine.

Trailhead

Bowen Ranch Road trailhead, near Hesperia, CA (private land access, no Adventure Pass needed there).
