Apple Valley

High-desert granite boulders near Apple Valley — a lesser-documented spot worth scouting with someone who's climbed there before.

Scattered granite boulder fields in the high desert around Apple Valley offer some lower-key bouldering, best treated as an exploratory trip rather than a well-documented destination. Bring a leader who's scouted the specific area recently, and expect to spend some time finding good lines.

High desert heat makes this a fall-through-spring destination rather than a summer one.

Trailhead

Access varies by specific boulder field — coordinate with a leader who's climbed here before.
