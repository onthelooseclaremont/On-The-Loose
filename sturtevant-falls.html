Hiking — San Gabriel Mountains, Big Santa Anita Canyon

Sturtevant Falls

A short, shaded, family-friendly hike to a genuine 50-foot waterfall — one of the easiest trips in the whole guide.

Sturtevant Falls is the easiest hike in this guide, and a good option for a low-commitment afternoon or a trip with less-experienced hikers. The trail follows Big Santa Anita Creek past a scattering of century-old private cabins to a genuine 50-foot waterfall and a shaded pool at its base.

It's popular — expect company, especially on weekends — but it's short enough to easily pair with a longer loop through the canyon (past Spruce Grove or up toward Mt. Wilson) for groups wanting more.

Trailhead

Chantry Flat, above Arcadia, CA — about 45 minutes from Claremont via the 210. Adventure Pass required.
