Hiking — San Gabriel Mountains (advanced)

Iron Mountain

Widely called the hardest day hike in the San Gabriels — brutal elevation gain, no water, and no shortage of reasons it's earned the reputation.

Iron Mountain has a reputation as the toughest day hike in the San Gabriels, and it earns it — the standard route via Heaton Flat (off Highway 39 / East Fork Road) gains roughly 6,000–7,200 feet over about 13–14 miles round trip, with a lot of steep, loose, brushy terrain and no reliable water source on route.

A second, far more serious option approaches from Mt. Baldy itself along the connecting ridge — this is an off-trail, route-finding traverse for experienced mountaineers only, not a standard day-hike option.

This is a leader-only, experienced-hikers-only route. Bring more water than seems reasonable, start before dawn, and set a hard turnaround time — the descent in the dark is genuinely dangerous.

Trailhead

East Fork Trailhead (Heaton Flat), off East Fork Road — same trailhead as Bridge to Nowhere. Adventure Pass + free wilderness permit required.
