Red Rocks

Dramatic red and white sandstone cliffs and badlands along Highway 14 — more of a hiking and scenery stop than a technical crag.

Red Rock Canyon State Park (not to be confused with the Nevada crag of the same nickname) is a striking stretch of eroded red and white cliffs along Highway 14 near Mojave — worth a stop for the scenery and some light scrambling and hiking through the badlands rather than technical roped climbing.

It's a good combination stop on the way to or from a longer desert trip further north.

Trailhead

Red Rock Canyon State Park Visitor Center, off Highway 14 near Mojave, CA — about 2.5 hours from Claremont.
