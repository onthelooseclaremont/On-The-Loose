Backpacking — Central Coast Ranges

Los Padres National Forest

A huge, varied stretch of coastal and interior mountains from Big Sur down to Ojai — one of the least crowded backpacking regions within reach.

Los Padres stretches along the coast from Big Sur down through the Santa Ynez and Sespe backcountry near Ojai — a huge, varied forest with far fewer visitors than the San Gabriels or Sierra. It's a good catch-all destination for OTL trips into less-trafficked terrain, from coastal condor country to interior chaparral wilderness.

Because the forest covers such a range of sub-regions, trip planning here usually starts with picking a specific trailhead or wilderness area (Sespe, Dick Smith, Matilija, Ventana) rather than "Los Padres" as a single destination.

Trailhead

Varies widely by sub-region — the Ojai Ranger District is the closest access point to Claremont, about 2 hours via the 210 and 33.
