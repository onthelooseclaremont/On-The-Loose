Anza-Borrego Desert

California's largest state park — badlands, slot canyons, and a spring wildflower bloom that draws crowds from across the state.

Anza-Borrego is the largest state park in California, and one of the only ones where you can camp almost anywhere off-road for free. It's a good beginner-friendly desert trip: slot canyons, palm oases, badlands, and (in a good year) a wildflower bloom worth planning a whole trip around.

Good introductory routes include the Borrego Palm Canyon hike to a fan-palm oasis, and a scramble through the narrows of Slot Canyon. Weather is mild and pleasant fall through spring; summer is genuinely dangerous heat and not a season to camp here.

Heads up: most of the park is free dispersed camping — no permit required — but that also means no water. Bring everything you'll need.

Trailhead

Borrego Springs, CA — visitor center off Palm Canyon Drive, about 2.5 hours from Claremont via the 10 and 86.
