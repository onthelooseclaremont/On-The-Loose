East Side of the Sierra

The 395 corridor: alpine lakes, hot springs, and some of the best high-altitude trailheads in California, all without the Yosemite crowds.

The Eastside is a catch-all for the string of trailheads along Highway 395 — Bishop, Big Pine, Mammoth Lakes, and points north — where the Sierra rises almost straight up out of the Owens Valley. It's a favorite for multi-day OTL trips because a short drive up any canyon road puts you at 9,000+ feet with a full menu of alpine lakes and passes.

Popular starting points include the North Lake and South Lake trailheads out of Bishop for the Sierra High Route and John Muir Wilderness access, and Mammoth Lakes for shorter day hikes and hot springs.

Trailhead

Depends on the trip — most groups base out of Bishop or Mammoth Lakes, both about 5 hours from Claremont via the 14 and 395.
